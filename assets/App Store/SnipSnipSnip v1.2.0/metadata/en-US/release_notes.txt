SnipSnipSnip 1.2.0 makes it easier to capture, explain, and share your work.

• Review Video first, then trim, remove sections, add zooms, refine cursor and click effects, and apply presentation styling. Source footage stays editable.
• Browse the redesigned floating Clipboard History palette, preview items in place, and use Copy and Return to resume work in your previous app.
• Use Capture Text to copy locally recognized words from your screen without opening a screenshot. Highlighter can fit strokes to recognized text.
• Use Capture Preview for quick post-capture actions and Output Size to choose the dimensions of delivered still images.
• Print screenshots, Comparisons, Steps, Combined Images, and existing Guides with File > Print.
• Find individual controls with Search Settings and jump directly to them.
• See which window will be selected before you click. Region and Window selection now share consistent targeting and support eligible dialogs and utility panels.
• Enjoy steadier annotation snapping and zoom, clearer output progress, safer document opening, and improved recovery reliability.

Clipboard behavior has changed: Auto Copy has been removed. Capturing and editing now leave the clipboard unchanged until you choose Copy. For quick capture-and-paste, use a Capture Preset with the Copy to Clipboard outcome. Explicit automation copy outputs still work.
