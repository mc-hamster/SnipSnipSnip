# SnipSnipSnip 1.1.1 App Store Screenshot Creative Brief

## Campaign Idea

**From screen capture to a story worth sharing.**

SnipSnipSnip is not presented as another utility that stops after taking a
screenshot. The sequence shows one continuous idea: capture something, make it
clear, turn it into a useful visual, find it again, and share it without giving
up privacy.

The first three screenshots must carry the product page on their own:

1. the broad promise,
2. the differentiating creation workflows,
3. the quality of the finished result.

The remaining screenshots deepen the story for people who continue browsing.

## Audience

- Developers and QA teams explaining changes and bugs
- Designers presenting interface work
- Technical writers and support teams building visual instructions
- Educators and creators recording and explaining their screens
- Mac power users who value capable, local-first tools

## Voice

- Direct, capable, and human
- Outcome-led rather than feature-led
- Short enough to scan at App Store thumbnail size
- Confident without claiming exclusivity or superiority
- Consistent with the canonical SnipSnipSnip workflow vocabulary

## Visual System

- Canvas: 1440 × 900, opaque RGB PNG
- Background: deep graphite with a subtle warm radial lift behind the hero
- Accent: restrained SnipSnipSnip orange for focus rings, paths, and small
  connective details
- Type: large white rounded display headline; quiet neutral supporting line
- Product imagery: real SnipSnipSnip Mac App Store UI only
- Demo content: attractive, privacy-safe, and intentionally authored for the
  campaign
- Composition: one dominant product moment per slide, with generous negative
  space and no small feature lists unless the list is the subject
- Continuity: a subtle orange thread or repeated crop edge can visually carry
  the viewer from Capture through Create, Polish, Find, and Share

Do not use decorative product-area colors, fake app interfaces, stock-device
mockups, watermarks, transparent output, or screenshots containing private
desktop content.

## Ten-Screenshot Story

### 01 — Promise

**Headline:** More Than a Screenshot.

**Supporting copy:** Capture, explain, polish, organize, and record—right on
your Mac.

**Hero:** The current Capture screen with Quick Capture, Create Something, and
Record visible. Layer two or three finished outputs behind it: a polished
Screenshot, a Comparison, and Steps.

**Purpose:** Establish breadth immediately without reading like a feature list.

### 02 — Create

**Headline:** Turn Screens Into Clear Stories.

**Supporting copy:** Create a Comparison, build Steps, or arrange a Combined
Image without leaving the app.

**Hero:** The Create Something controls above sharp, full-size Comparison and
Steps outcomes. Combined Image remains visible alongside them in the control
row.

**Purpose:** Lead with the major workflow difference that the older campaign
does not show.

### 03 — Polish

**Headline:** Make Every Screenshot Presentation-Ready.

**Supporting copy:** Add spacing, backgrounds, framing, shadows, and Mockups
before you copy or export.

**Hero:** A strong before/after treatment: the edited Screenshot on the left
and its Polish preview on the right, with the Polish inspector visible.

**Purpose:** Show why the finished result looks intentional without requiring a
separate design app.

### 04 — Edit

**Headline:** Change Anything. Flatten Nothing.

**Supporting copy:** Crop, annotate, highlight, redact, arrange, and undo while
the Screenshot stays editable.

**Hero:** A focused editor view with a small, coherent set of annotations and
the Change History destination visible. The annotation content should explain
the screenshot rather than merely decorate it.

**Purpose:** Communicate non-destructive depth in a memorable phrase.

### 05 — What Changed

**Headline:** Share What Changed.

**Supporting copy:** Export one offline HTML file with interactive comparison
controls.

**Hero:** The in-app Comparison review beside the actual exported Interactive
HTML report. The report should visibly include Compare Using, zoom, Highlight
Changes, and result-visibility controls.

**Purpose:** Show QA, design, and development audiences that “what changed” can
be shared as an interactive, self-contained offline report—not only as a flat
image.

### 06 — Steps

**Headline:** Explain It One Step at a Time.

**Supporting copy:** Add, order, and caption screenshots as clear visual Steps.

**Hero:** A polished three- or four-step sequence for a believable Mac task,
with concise captions and visible numbering.

**Purpose:** Show that SnipSnipSnip creates documentation, not just isolated
images.

### 07 — Find

**Headline:** Find Any Screenshot Fast.

**Supporting copy:** Search Snip History by filename, annotation, and text
recognized locally on your Mac.

**Hero:** Snip Library search results for a purposeful query, with the matching
text visible in a selected result preview.

**Purpose:** Connect capture volume to retrieval and reinforce local OCR.

### 08 — Clipboard

**Headline:** Your Clipboard Has a Memory.

**Supporting copy:** Search, preview, and reuse screenshots, text, files, and
more.

**Hero:** Clipboard History with search and filters, a varied history list, and
the selected item's full preview and metadata.

**Purpose:** Broaden the daily-use story beyond screenshots and show the
history itself instead of its settings.

### 09 — Video

**Headline:** Record. Trim. Share.

**Supporting copy:** Record a Region, Window, or Screen with audio, then trim
and export the Video.

**Hero:** The Video editor at a meaningful trim point, with a clean timeline,
poster frame, and clear source selector.

**Purpose:** Complete the screen-communication story with motion.

### 10 — Tools

**Headline:** Measure Twice. Capture Once.

**Supporting copy:** Check dimensions, colors, pixels, and spacing without
leaving your screen.

**Hero:** Screen Ruler measuring a vivid image beside Screen Inspector's live
pixel loupe and color readout.

**Purpose:** End with two visually distinctive power tools that make the app
feel broader than a conventional screenshot editor.

## Capture Plan

Create a clean demo library before the capture pass so every screenshot feels
like part of the same campaign. Use one fictional project across the editor,
Comparison, Steps, Snip Library, Clipboard History, and Video where practical.
That continuity makes the sequence read as a story instead of ten unrelated
feature demonstrations.

Required product states:

1. Empty Capture screen with Quick Capture, Create Something, and Record
2. Screenshot in Edit with purposeful annotations and Change History
3. The same Screenshot in Polish with Look or Mockup configured
4. Comparison in Review using Wipe or Highlight Changes
5. Steps in Order & Caption
6. Combined Image in Arrange
7. Snip Library search with a useful local OCR match
8. Clipboard History browser with safe demo items
9. Video in Trim
10. Screen Ruler and Screen Inspector

## Production Workflow

1. Approve the story and exact copy.
2. Author privacy-safe demo content.
3. Capture the required SnipSnipSnip 1.1.1 states from one controlled build.
4. Compose all ten slides from a shared deterministic template.
5. Review the full contact sheet for rhythm, legibility, claim accuracy, and
   visual repetition.
6. Export opaque 1440 × 900 PNG files using ordered, App Store-ready names.
7. Verify dimensions, color mode, alpha absence, and text spelling
   automatically.

## Edition Guardrails

This campaign is for the Mac App Store edition. Do not show or claim live Guide
creation, Scrolling Capture, Connected Device Capture, or UI Map capture.
Those workflows belong to SnipSnipSnip Pro.
