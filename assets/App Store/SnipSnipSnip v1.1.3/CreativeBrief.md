# SnipSnipSnip 1.1.3 App Store Screenshot Creative Brief

## Campaign Idea

**From screen capture to a story worth sharing.**

SnipSnipSnip is not presented as another utility that stops after taking a
screenshot. The sequence shows one continuous idea: capture something, make it
clear, turn it into a useful visual, and share it without giving up privacy.

The first three screenshots must carry the product page on their own:

1. the broad promise,
2. the differentiating creation workflows,
3. the quality of the finished result.

The remaining six screenshots deepen the story for people who continue
browsing.

## Audience

- Developers and QA teams explaining changes and bugs
- Designers presenting interface work
- Technical writers and support teams building visual instructions
- Educators and creators recording and explaining their screens
- Mac power users who value capable, local-first tools

## Voice

- Direct, capable, and human
- Outcome-led rather than feature-led
- Short enough to scan at App Store thumbnail size
- Confident without claiming exclusivity or superiority
- Consistent with the canonical SnipSnipSnip workflow vocabulary

## Visual System

- Canvas: 1440 × 900, opaque RGB PNG
- Background: warm parchment with restrained orange and cool-blue atmospheric
  lifts. It must remain visibly distinct from the app's light window at
  thumbnail size.
- Accent: restrained SnipSnipSnip orange for the eyebrow marker, rule, and
  small connective details
- Type: large near-black rounded display headline; quiet warm-gray supporting
  line
- Product stage: the product image fills its allotted area directly, without a
  synthetic card, border, frame, or shadow. Preserve the actual App Store UI;
  do not recolor or regenerate app pixels.
- Product imagery: real SnipSnipSnip Mac App Store UI only
- Demo content: attractive, privacy-safe, and intentionally authored for the
  campaign
- Composition: one dominant product moment per slide, with generous negative
  space and no small feature lists unless the list is the subject
- Continuity: a subtle orange thread carries the viewer from Capture through
  Create, Polish, Review, and Arrange
- Sequence: keep filenames ordered for upload, but do not display slide numbers
  in the artwork

Do not use decorative product-area colors, fake app interfaces, stock-device
mockups, watermarks, transparent output, or screenshots containing private
desktop content.

## Nine-Screenshot Story

### 01 — Promise

**Headline:** More Than a Screenshot.

**Supporting copy:** Capture, explain, polish, and organize—right on your Mac.

**Hero:** The current Capture screen with Quick Capture, Create, Record, Screen
Tools, and Clipboard History visible.

**Purpose:** Establish breadth immediately without reading like a feature list.

### 02 — Create

**Headline:** Turn Screens Into Clear Stories.

**Supporting copy:** Create a Comparison, build Steps, or arrange a Combined
Image without leaving the app.

**Hero:** The Create sheet with Screenshot, Comparison, Steps, and Combined
Image choices plus their available sources.

**Purpose:** Lead with the major workflow difference that the older campaign
does not show.

### 03 — Polish

**Headline:** Make It Presentation-Ready.

**Supporting copy:** Add spacing, backgrounds, framing, shadows, and Mockups
before you copy or export.

**Hero:** The Screenshot in Polish with Look and Mockup choices plus the
Background and Effects controls visible.

**Purpose:** Show why the finished result looks intentional without requiring a
separate design app.

### 04 — Edit

**Headline:** Change Anything. Flatten Nothing.

**Supporting copy:** Crop, annotate, highlight, redact, arrange, and undo while
the Screenshot stays editable.

**Hero:** A focused editor view with the full annotation toolbar, editable
selection handles, and Properties and History destinations visible.

**Purpose:** Communicate non-destructive depth in a memorable phrase.

### 05 — Comparison

**Headline:** Share What Changed.

**Supporting copy:** Review two versions side by side, highlight changes, and
export the result.

**Hero:** Comparison in Review with the Before and After images and comparison
controls visible.

**Purpose:** Show QA, design, and development audiences that differences can be
reviewed and shared as a deliberate visual result.

### 06 — Steps

**Headline:** Explain It One Step at a Time.

**Supporting copy:** Add, order, and caption screenshots as clear visual Steps.

**Hero:** A two-step sequence with Order & Caption controls and numbering,
caption, connector, and flow options visible.

**Purpose:** Show that SnipSnipSnip creates documentation, not just isolated
images.

### 07 — Combined Image

**Headline:** Bring Every View Together.

**Supporting copy:** Arrange multiple images into one clear, polished Combined
Image.

**Hero:** Combined Image in Arrange with multiple images and layout choices
visible.

**Purpose:** End with a clear example of several captures becoming one useful
output.

### 08 — Clipboard History

**Headline:** Your Clipboard Has a Memory.

**Supporting copy:** Search, preview, pin, and reuse text, links, files, images,
and Screenshots.

**Hero:** Clipboard History with privacy-safe text, link, file, color, JSON, and
Screenshot examples. Keep one Screenshot selected so its full preview and
metadata are visible.

**Purpose:** Show how SnipSnipSnip extends the daily workflow beyond captures
without turning the slide into a generic feature list.

### 09 — Screen Tools

**Headline:** Measure Twice. Capture Once.

**Supporting copy:** Check dimensions, colors, pixels, and spacing without
leaving your screen.

**Hero:** A horizontal Screen Ruler over the app beside Screen Inspector's
pixel loupe, crosshair, grid, coordinates, color values, and measurement
controls.

**Purpose:** End with two visually distinctive Screen Tools that reinforce the
app's value as a complete screen-work companion.

## Capture Plan

Use one privacy-safe demo project across the editor, Comparison, Steps, and
Combined Image states. That continuity makes the sequence read as a story
instead of unrelated feature demonstrations.

Required product states:

1. Empty Capture screen with Quick Capture, Create, Record, Screen Tools, and
   Clipboard History
2. Create sheet with workflow and source choices
3. Screenshot in Polish with Look, Mockup, Background, and Effects controls
4. Screenshot in Edit with the annotation toolbar and editable selection
5. Comparison in Review
6. Steps in Order & Caption
7. Combined Image in Arrange
8. Clipboard History with varied privacy-safe demo items
9. Screen Ruler and Screen Inspector over the privacy-safe Capture screen

## Production Workflow

1. Approve the story and exact copy.
2. Author privacy-safe demo content.
3. Capture the required SnipSnipSnip 1.1.3 (156) states from one controlled
   app-host process with `APP_STORE_BUILD` enabled, and validate the Release
   App Store build separately.
4. Compose all nine slides from a shared deterministic template.
5. Review the full contact sheet for rhythm, legibility, claim accuracy, and
   visual repetition.
6. Export opaque 1440 × 900 PNG files using ordered, App Store-ready names.
7. Verify dimensions, color mode, alpha absence, and text spelling
   automatically.

## Edition Guardrails

This campaign is for the Mac App Store edition. Do not show or claim live Guide
creation, Scrolling Capture, Connected Device Capture, or UI Map capture.
Those workflows belong to SnipSnipSnip Pro.
